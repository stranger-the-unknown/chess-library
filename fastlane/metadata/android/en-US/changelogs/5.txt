Version 4.0.0

Analysis
- Batch analysis: pick games from a list, start a quick or deep analysis
  and leave the phone. The screen stays on while it works, so it is best
  done on charge.
- Two new lists at the top: the last 100 deep analyses and the last 100
  quick ones, newest first. They cannot be deleted or renamed.
- Opening a saved analysis no longer re-runs the engine.
- Marking an analysis read or favourite also marks the original game.

Game lists
- Both player names now fit on the card, and the date is shown when the
  PGN has one.
- "Game details" in the row menu: event, site, round, ECO.
- Sorting can be reversed.
- Number range filter: show only games in a given range.
- The read count now appears on the list card.

Fixes
- The last row of a list could sit under the phone's navigation bar.
- Marking one game as read could mark a different game; imported games
  shared ids. Existing lists are repaired on startup.
- App sounds stopped your music without letting it resume; they now mix.
- Typing a single digit in search returned unrelated results.
- Filters stay put instead of scrolling when they fit.

Other
- "Reset all data" in settings.
- The window opens maximised on Windows.
