Version 3.1

Board colours
- Ready-made boards are gone. You now pick the light and dark square
  colour yourself from a palette of 100; the board is drawn from those
  two colours.
- The colour screen shows a preview of the real board — pieces and
  square names included — so you see your choice immediately.
- Wood grain is no longer tied to three fixed boards; it is a switch
  that lays a faint grain over whichever colours you chose.
- Every square colour from the previous fifteen boards is in the
  palette, so no look was lost, and your old setting is converted
  automatically.
- Square names: if you pick two colours that are too close, the
  contrast is measured and the text falls back to black or white so it
  never disappears.

Piece sets
- Seven more: Cburnett, Merida, Mono, Letter, Pirouetti, Pixel,
  MPChess. Sixteen in total.

Licence
- The app is now AGPLv3. Some of the new piece sets are GPL/AGPL
  licensed, which requires it. The app stays open source and commercial
  use stays allowed; in return, anyone who receives the app can get its
  source, and modified versions must be distributed under the same
  licence. Version 3.0.0 and earlier remain MIT.

Fix
- When restoring a backup, a setting missing from the backup kept its
  old value on the device; it now returns to the default.
