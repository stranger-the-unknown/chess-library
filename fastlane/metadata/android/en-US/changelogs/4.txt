Version 3.0

Backup
- Everything the app stores can be written to a single file and restored:
  game lists, puzzles and your progress, openings, settings. The file is
  plain text, so it carries between phone and computer as it is.
- Before restoring, the file's contents are summarised; there are two
  modes, merge and replace.

Puzzles
- Numbers are now independent of filters: the number shown is the one
  the puzzle has in the list.
- Lists are ordered newest first; the arrow in the title bar flips it.
- Mark a range of numbers solved or unsolved in one go.
- White wins / draw / black wins filters on endgame lists.
- Count of puzzles solved today (can be turned off in settings).

Game lists
- Favourites, and marking a range of games read or unread.

Openings
- Added variations can be edited, several variations can live under one
  family, and they import and export as text files.

Board
- Nine piece sets and fifteen boards.
- On the desktop, right-click marks a square and right-drag draws an
  arrow.

Fixes
- The first move after a cold start made no sound.
- The board was invisible when "Set up position" was opened from the
  play-the-engine dialog.
- The illegal-move sound now only plays while you are in check.
