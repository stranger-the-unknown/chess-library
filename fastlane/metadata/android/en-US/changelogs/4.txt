Version 3.0

Boards
- Seventeen new boards on top of the fifteen, thirty-two in total:
  four woods, two maples, marble, blue marble, stone, metal, leather,
  canvas, olive, green plastic, pink pyramid, purple diagonal and
  horsey. These are real texture images, usable now that the licence
  is AGPLv3.
- Square names: on boards like stone and marble, where the two square
  colours are close, the text used to disappear into the background.
  The contrast is now measured and falls back to black or white.

Piece sets
- Seven more: Cburnett, Merida, Mono, Letter, Pirouetti, Pixel,
  MPChess. Sixteen in total.

Licence
- The app is now AGPLv3. Some of the new piece sets are GPL/AGPL
  licensed, which requires it. The app stays open source and commercial
  use stays allowed; in return, anyone who receives the app can get its
  source, and modified versions must be distributed under the same
  licence. Version 3.0.0 and earlier remain MIT.

Fix
- When restoring a backup, a setting missing from the backup kept its
  old value on the device; it now returns to the default.

Backup
- Everything the app stores can be written to a single file and restored:
  game lists, puzzles and your progress, openings, settings. The file is
  plain text, so it carries between phone and computer as it is.
- Before restoring, the file's contents are summarised; there are two
  modes, merge and replace.

Puzzles
- Numbers are now independent of filters: the number shown is the one
  the puzzle has in the list.
- The arrow in the title bar flips the list to newest first.
- Mark a range of numbers solved or unsolved in one go.
- White wins / draw / black wins filters on endgame lists.
- Count of puzzles solved today (can be turned off in settings).

Game lists
- Favourites, and marking a range of games read or unread.

Openings
- Added variations can be edited, several variations can live under one
  family, and they import and export as text files.

Board
- Nine piece sets and fifteen boards.
- On the desktop, right-click marks a square and right-drag draws an
  arrow.

Fixes
- The first move after a cold start made no sound.
- The board was invisible when "Set up position" was opened from the
  play-the-engine dialog.
- The illegal-move sound now only plays while you are in check.
