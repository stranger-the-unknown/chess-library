Chess Library is a chess app that works entirely on your device. There is no
account, no network access and no tracking — nothing ever leaves your phone.

<b>Play</b>
Play against a built-in engine with six difficulty levels, from beginner to
master. Pick your colour, or let it be random, and start from the normal setup
or from a position you build yourself. The engine runs in a separate thread,
so the board stays smooth.

<b>Read your games</b>
Load PGN files or paste text. A single file may hold any number of games;
pick the ones you want and save them as a new list or add them to an existing
one. Headers, comments, variations, NAG marks and games starting from a FEN
are all read. Lists are numbered and searchable, and you can mark games as
read.

<b>Review</b>
When a game ends the engine evaluates every move: accuracy per side, move
quality (best, excellent, good, inaccuracy, mistake, blunder), an evaluation
graph and the turning points of the game. While looking at a saved game you
can also try a different move and watch how the evaluation changes — those
trial moves are never written into the game.

<b>Puzzles</b>
The app ships with no puzzles; you build the lists yourself. Paste FENs, add
positions one by one, or import a text file with thousands of them. Lists can
be exported back to a text file for backup. Where a solution is known the
recorded line is used; otherwise the engine judges your move, so any move
that keeps the advantage counts as correct.

<b>Openings</b>
Add the lines you want to study by name and moves; every move is checked
against the rules. Watch a line step by step, or practise it and get told
when you go wrong.

<b>Made to look right</b>
Sixteen piece sets and thirty-two board styles. Square coordinates take their colour
from the board you choose, so they stay readable everywhere. Boards can be
saved as PNG images.

Interface languages: Turkish, English.
